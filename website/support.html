Frequently asked questions

Is Dishly free?

Yes. Dishly is a free meal planner and grocery list app.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to use Dishly.

Can I use Dishly offline?

Yes. Meal planning and grocery list features are designed for offline use.

How do I change a planned meal?

Open your weekly plan, select the meal you want to change, and use the recipe swap option to choose another meal.

What should I do before deleting the app?

Keep a separate copy of any plans or lists you want to preserve. Deleting an app can remove its locally saved information; do not assume reinstalling will restore it. Offloading and device backups behave differently from deletion.

Basic troubleshooting

  1. Close Dishly and reopen it.
  2. Restart your device if the issue continues.
  3. Check the App Store for an available Dishly update.
  4. If it still happens, email the steps that caused the issue and any error message.

Avoid deleting and reinstalling Dishly as a first troubleshooting step, because locally saved information may be lost.
