Dishly is described as a free offline meal planner and grocery list app. This draft explains its intended handling of information and separately describes the verified behavior of this website.

Accounts

According to the app description supplied by the developer, an account is not required to use Dishly.

Meal preferences, plans, and grocery lists

Dishly’s intended offline design saves meal preferences, weekly plans, recipe selections, and grocery lists on your device so you can use them without an internet connection. The exact storage mechanisms, saved fields, retention behavior, and any transmission outside the device still require verification against the release app.

Local information and deletion

Locally saved information may remain until you change or remove it, or delete the app. Deleting an iOS app generally removes its app-local data, but offloading the app can preserve data. Device or cloud backups, restored backups, and any storage outside the app’s ordinary local container can affect what remains or returns. Dishly’s precise backup and deletion behavior must be confirmed before this policy becomes effective.

Third-party services and network activity

No app source, dependency list, or runtime network trace was available for this draft. Analytics, crash reporting, updates, remote recipe content or images, advertising, and other SDK activity therefore remain unverified. This draft does not assert that Dishly collects no data, makes no network requests, or uses no third-party services. When you contact support
